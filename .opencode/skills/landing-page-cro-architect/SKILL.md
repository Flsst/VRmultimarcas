# Landing Page CRO Architect

Treat a landing page as a guided decision journey.

## Start with the conversion model

Identify:
- target visitor;
- traffic intent;
- awareness level;
- desired action;
- offer;
- main objection;
- strongest proof;
- conversion friction.

Do not invent facts to fill gaps.

## Above the fold

Within the first screen, strive to communicate:
- what this is;
- who it is for;
- why it matters;
- what action to take.

Keep the primary CTA unmistakable. A secondary CTA is acceptable only if it serves a distinct visitor intent.

## Section sequencing

Build the sequence from visitor questions rather than a generic template.

Useful modules include:
- value proposition;
- product/service showcase;
- benefits;
- how it works;
- differentiators;
- credibility;
- reviews/testimonials when real;
- portfolio/cases;
- financing or purchase conditions when real;
- FAQ/objection handling;
- location/contact;
- final CTA.

Choose only what supports the sale.

## CTA discipline

- Use consistent action language.
- Place CTAs after meaningful persuasion moments.
- Avoid competing primary actions.
- On mobile, consider a sticky CTA only when it improves access without obscuring content.
- For WhatsApp or phone actions, make the expected next step clear.

## Copy structure

Prefer specific benefits over vague prestige language.
Avoid:
- “innovation that transforms”;
- “excellence and quality” without proof;
- generic superlatives;
- fabricated urgency;
- fabricated scarcity;
- fake metrics.

Headlines should carry the argument; body copy should support it.

## Trust

Trust may come from:
- real reviews;
- known clients;
- warranties;
- certifications;
- years in business;
- physical location;
- transparent process;
- clear policies;
- real inventory/product information;
- credible photography.

Only use evidence provided by the project or available source material.

## Review

Before completion, inspect the page as a skeptical visitor:
- Why should I care?
- Why this company?
- Can I trust it?
- What happens after I click?
- Is there any unnecessary friction?
- Is the CTA visible when I am ready to act?

Reorder or rewrite sections when the journey is weak.
